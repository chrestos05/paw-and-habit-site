The idea

Better habits for happier pets.

Paw & Habit began with a simple observation: the most useful pet advice is not always the loudest. It is the advice that fits into an ordinary home, an ordinary schedule, and the individual animal in front of us.

We create calm, practical resources around enrichment, behavior, play, and wellbeing — starting with indoor cats and expanding carefully over time.

Our approach is animal-centered and non-gimmicky. We focus on choice, observation, safety, and small changes that can become lasting habits.

What we believe

Enrichment is a need, not a luxury. Cats need opportunities to move, forage, scratch, climb, explore, and retreat.

Every animal is an individual. The best plan is one that respects temperament, age, mobility, and preference.

Good design reduces friction. A beautiful routine is useful only when it is easy to repeat.

For cats who live indoors — and the people who want to understand them better.
