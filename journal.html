A note on our content

Careful, useful, honest.

Is Paw & Habit veterinary advice?

No. Our content is educational and enrichment-focused. Health concerns and sudden behavior changes belong with a qualified veterinarian.

How does Paw & Habit earn money?

Through digital products and, when active, clearly disclosed affiliate relationships. Affiliate links never increase the customer’s price.
